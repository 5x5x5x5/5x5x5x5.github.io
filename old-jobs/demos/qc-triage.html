    Automated by System

    • Parse Illumina bcl2fastq Stats.json and FastQC reports deterministically (zero LLM arithmetic).
    • Detect lane-level batch defects (§5.1), Read 2 phasing drops (§3.2), pool dropouts (§3.4), and biological assay exceptions (§4.2).
    • Attach verbatim SOP-NGS-042 citations and emit split transfer_manifest.csv / quarantine_manifest.csv.

    Stays Human & Where Automation Fails

    • Stays human: Every ESCALATE call, wet-lab re-sequencing repeats, sponsor waivers, and external site handoff.
    • Where it fails: Upstream LIMS assay_type mislabeling, QC-invisible sample swaps (requires alignment/SNP fingerprinting), and novel optical artifacts outside SOP-NGS-042.
