Questions a Peer or Client Usually Asks

Why are some datasets public and some synthetic?

No proprietary, clinical, or member-identifiable data is ever placed in a public repo. Stops 1, 3, 4, and 6 use real public datasets (Fishnet Open Images, PubChem AID1851, MIT-licensed MultiQC Illumina runs, and the 28-chapter TAIHLS textbook). Stops 2, 5, 8, and 9 use explicitly labeled synthetic fixtures to mirror GMP, privacy, and CRO boundaries honestly.

Why emphasize deterministic cores over pure LLM agents?

In regulated biology and operations, asking an LLM to do arithmetic, unit conversion, or send unattended emails is an engineering defect. Every system here pushes arithmetic, schema validation, and safety gates into tested Python—and uses models where language judgment actually earns its keep.

How does this translate to consulting engagements?

Through Possible Futures, LLC, I design, build, and evaluate applied AI systems—especially for biotech, clinical labs, and life-sciences teams. Each stop demonstrates how quickly a domain-grounded prototype with real eval gates can be shipped.
