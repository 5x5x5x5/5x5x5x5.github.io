Where It Fails (Honest Limits)

  • Minority-class collapse on PubMedQA: neither base nor 2-epoch SFT predicted `maybe` (0/12 gold items) without class weighting/oversampling.
  • Autoregressive contradiction (`pubid 12068831`): emitted `verdict: yes` before writing a rationale concluding no evidence existed — solved by generating `rationale` before `verdict`.
  • Unmerged 4-bit LoRA adapters halved T4 throughput (96.3 → 44.9 tok/s), and N=100 yielded borderline p=0.064 (proving larger eval sets + adapter merging are required before production).
