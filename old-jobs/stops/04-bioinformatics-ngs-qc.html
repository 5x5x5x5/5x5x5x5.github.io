Human vs. Machine Boundary

Automated by ngs-qc-triage

  • Deterministic parsing of real Illumina bcl2fastq Stats.json and FastQC archives (zero LLM arithmetic).
  • Assay-aware rules (exempting expected duplication in targeted panels/amplicons while escalating WGS GC contamination, Read 2 phasing drops, and lane-wide defects).
  • Hybrid BM25 + char n-gram SOP-NGS-042 retrieval with verbatim clause citations, MCP server, and split transfer/quarantine CSV manifests.

Stays Human

  • Resolving every ESCALATE flag (GC contamination, Read 2 phasing drops, >20% undetermined reads, corrupted files).
  • Authorizing wet-lab re-sequencing or library-prep repeats.
  • Sponsor waivers and external communication with the satellite lab.
