Fisheries Observer

In Progress deck-observer Build time: In progress (active branch)

Human fisheries observers cannot provide 100% haul coverage across commercial longline vessels at sea, yet catch accounting and protected-species bycatch depend on species-level tallies as fish come over the rail.

visionedgemeasurement
Repository in active local development

Human vs. Machine Boundary

Automated by deck-observer

  • Detect each fish and person in rail/chute electronic monitoring (EM) frames with coarse taxonomic grouping.
  • Crop detected fish for fine-grained species classification with confidence-based abstention.
  • Emit structured observer tally sheets and route uncertain/rare frames for review.

Stays Human

  • Final species verification on occluded, rare, or protected bycatch (e.g., seabirds/rockfish).
  • Biological sampling: otolith extraction, length/sex measurements, and physical deck inspection.
  • Regulatory compliance judgment and vessel debriefing.

Where It Fails (Honest Limits)

  • Domain shift: Fishnet captures western/central Pacific tuna longline gear rather than Alaska groundfish species until AFSC data arrives.
  • Motion blur, salt spray, and overlapping fish on the rail degrade single-frame confidence (requiring tracking + human abstention).
  • Stage 1 training and edge-CPU FPS benchmarks are actively underway in a parallel session; no trained mAP numbers are claimed yet.
