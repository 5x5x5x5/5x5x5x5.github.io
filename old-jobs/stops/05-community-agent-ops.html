Human vs. Machine Boundary

Automated by dlrtp-ops-demo

  • Herald: deterministic post-PR fan-out (ICS, Discord embed, manual paste payload; zero LLM at runtime).
  • Funnel: untrusted inbound triage into quarantined queues with zero send credentials in context (architectural prompt-injection defense).
  • Radar + Scribe + Ledger: anti-vendor policy filter, human-seeded recap drafts, and heartbeat/freshness dead-man switches.

Stays Human

  • Running the room and picking the three surprising takeaways (Scribe refuses empty human seeds).
  • Every relationship speech act (champion asks, lapsed-regular check-ins, thank-yous—Funnel refuses to auto-draft).
  • Moderation, consent, and final publish/paste gates (~3 content-object gates/week).

Where It Fails (Honest Limits)

  • Subtle vendor pitches disguised as technical talks still need human review in the weekly Radar PR.
  • Meetup and personal LinkedIn resist APIs; last-mile delivery intentionally stops at clipboard paste rather than brittle browser automation.
  • Production `dlrtp-ops` remains private to protect real member emails and attendance; this public repo runs on 100% synthetic fixtures.
