Human vs. Machine Boundary

Automated by batchlens

  • Join upstream culture signals (VCD, IVCD, specific productivity, harvest titer) with downstream purification steps (Protein A through UF/DF).
  • Quantify exactly where a batch diverged from the reference batch and what that divergence cost in grams.
  • Detect incomplete/invalid batch records (`BL-0005`) and refuse to fabricate a yield number.

Stays Human

  • Causal root-cause attribution and GMP deviation / CAPA sign-off.
  • Batch disposition and release decisions.
  • Bioreactor or chromatography floor intervention.
