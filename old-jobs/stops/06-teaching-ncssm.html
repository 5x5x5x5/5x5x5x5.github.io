Human vs. Machine Boundary

Automated by taihls-feedback

  • BM25 retrieval over all 28 chapters of the open TAIHLS textbook (taihls.com).
  • Misconception detection paired with Socratic revision hints and a strict anti-answer-leak guard.
  • Automatic teacher escalation for confused, off-rubric, or help-seeking submissions — paired with interactive static webpage explainers (gradient descent, k-means, spaced repetition) and the `duck` voice sparring partner.

Stays Human

  • Final grading, nuanced partial credit, and classroom facilitation.
  • Mentoring and spotting quiet disengagement.
  • Instructor review of all escalated submissions and editorial review of TAIHLS chapters.
