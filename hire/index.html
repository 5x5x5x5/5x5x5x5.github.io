FAQ

Frequently anticipated questions.

Is there an API?

Yes — email. It's REST-ish: you send a request, and Daniel returns a response. Rate limits apply. Webhooks are called "calendar invites."

Can I upgrade mid-cycle?

Instantly. Downgrades take one sprint, for emotional reasons.

What does fine-tuning look like?

~2 weeks of onboarding on your codebase and infra. Catastrophic forgetting of your legacy Perl is a feature, not a bug.

Do you offer a free trial?

A 15-minute chat — grab a slot. Output is watermarked ("as I mentioned on our call…").

PyTorch or JAX?

Fluent PyTorch, increasingly JAX. Will jit anything that holds still long enough. Functional where practical, pragmatic where not.

Is this a joke?

The pricing table is real. The costume is the joke. Attention really is the scarce resource — someone proved that in 2017.
