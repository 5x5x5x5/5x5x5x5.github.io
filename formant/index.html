FORMANT FLYER

Your ship is a tongue. Its engine sings. Fly the IPA vowel chart.

  • Move: mouse, drag, or arrows/WASD. Left–right is front–back, up–down is close–open.
  • Lips: Space, click, or the LIPS button toggles spread ⇄ rounded.
  • Park on a target's landing ring until it shatters. Cyan ellipse = spread, magenta circle = rounded, gold diamond = either.
  • Ear mode: a ? sings its vowel once (the ship ducks). Fly to that vowel before it lands; misses show where it was and sing it again.
  • Consonants: place across, manner down; Space toggles voicing. Clear a whole row or column for a bonus; sweep the ʘ ǀ ǁ ǃ ǂ click trail. Dodge ‖ pauses.
  • Dodge ʔ glottal-stop mines. Trace gold ring trails to sing diphthongs.
