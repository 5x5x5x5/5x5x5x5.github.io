    HOW TO PLAY

    1. Each table is an hour. Tap a table to cycle your answer: ☕ in → ? maybe → out.
    2. Press PASS TO … and text the link to that friend (or the group chat). It carries everyone's answers so far.
    3. Always open the newest link. Your phone remembers every link you've opened, so nothing gets lost.
    4. When all four have answered and some hour has nobody out, the quest is won: the hour with the most ☕, earliest on ties.

    Keyboard: arrows or WASD move, space or Enter changes your answer.
